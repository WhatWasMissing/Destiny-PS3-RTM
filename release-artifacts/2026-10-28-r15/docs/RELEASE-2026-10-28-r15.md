# Destiny RTM prerelease — 2026-10-28 revision 15

R15 adds a read-only comparison for the latest two complete live BLES 01.24
item-pool snapshots. It compares pool slot and item index only, and enables the
comparison only when the connected RTM session and item-pool allocation remain
the same. SOIDs and owner identifiers do not appear in results. The samples
remain sequential and non-atomic; equal item indexes do not distinguish item
instances, and this diagnostic does not verify transfer or persistence.

The live inventory mutation route is still unresolved. The Blackrock inventory
callback reaches a delayed confirmation path, but its final transport and the
BLES item-action handler have not been linked. The existing Blackrock spawn
executor remains experimental; live spawn behavior was not validated in this
build pass.

The reconnect source audit found no PS3MAPI service stop/start path. Health
monitoring probes only the read-only `SERVER GETVERSION` endpoint and updates
RTM connection state. With auto reconnect enabled, intermittent outages can
lead to another UI relink cycle after a probe succeeds. The development PS3 was
unreachable during the last reported probe (ports 80 and 7887), so live
connection behavior remains unverified.
